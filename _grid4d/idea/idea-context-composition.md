# Context composition

> The main value is the community of unique individuals; unique abilities grow from a context earned by a long history of own experience - and the full context of any work is the composition of instance, session, Epic and story properties.

**Status:** open
**Source:** the Human, 2026-10-31 (consolidation session, [AHA-0017](AHA-0017))

## Idea

- **The community is the value.** The point of ahabase is a collaborating community of unique individuals with unique abilities - more successful together than individuals apart ([AHABASE](AHABASE), Vision).
- **Uniqueness is grown.** Unique abilities arise from a context that can only be earned through a long history of one's own experience - not transferred, not bought, not promptable.
- **Context is composed.** The full context of a piece of work is the combination of four layers' properties: the **instance** (its environment and history), the **session** (its conversation and memory), the **Epic** (its knowledge and rules) and the **story** (its goal and state).

Consequences to shape: context should be carried and composed explicitly instead of being lost between layers; collaboration should pair contexts that differ, because the difference is the asset ([idea-cross-instance-context-view](idea-cross-instance-context-view)).

**Where it stands.** The four layers exist as structures in the method (the instance registry, session bindings, Epic knowledge, story state); composing them into one carried context is the direction, not yet built.
