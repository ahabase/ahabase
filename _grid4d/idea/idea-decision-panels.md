# Decision panels

> Visualize and document the scorecards and diagrams a story's decisions are actually made on - every panel a numbered story document, so each decision is documented where it was made.

**Status:** open
**Source:** the Human, 2026-10-31 (consolidation session, [AHA-0017](AHA-0017)) - named the current main task

## Idea

During a story, decisions are made on instruments - scorecards and diagrams - that today live in sessions and disappear with them. The direction: make the panels first-class.

- **Visualize** the panels decisions are made on: the scorecards (state, debt, progress, cost, review) and the diagrams (structure, flow, options weighed).
- **Document** each panel as a numbered story document, so every decision made on it is documented and traceable ([idea-decision-driven-stories](idea-decision-driven-stories)).
- **The owning layer is being incubated.** The method's decision subepic (founded on the Human's yes of 2026-10-30) is exactly this: diagrams for decisions and scorecards for choosing among options, with its own ontology (situation, question, option, criterion, consequence, method, decision, diagram-type, supersession behind an index), a decision-record template and a projections pattern. The base-side question this idea carries: which parts graduate into this package when proven ([graduation-criteria](graduation-criteria)), and what a decision panel looks like as a numbered story document here.
