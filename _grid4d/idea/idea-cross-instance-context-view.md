# Cross-instance context view

> Collaboration across instances adds a view through another instance's context - its instructions, its history, its ontology blueprints, possibly other models behind it.

**Status:** open
**Source:** the Human, 2026-10-31 (consolidation session, [AHA-0017](AHA-0017))

## Idea

Cross-instance collaboration is valuable precisely because the other instance sees the work through a different context: another instance's instructions to its agents, its accumulated history, its ontology blueprints - and possibly other models doing the seeing. The direction: treat the difference of contexts as a first-class asset of collaboration - visible, comparable, usable - rather than an accident to be hidden.

**Where it stands.** The collaboration itself is implemented in the method (its collaboration subepic: cross-instance channels, presence, receipts, review rounds). The view through the other instance's context - its instructions, history, blueprints, models - is the part not yet made first-class; that is this idea.
