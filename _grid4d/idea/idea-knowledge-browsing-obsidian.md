# Knowledge browsing: Obsidian or the classic way

> Open question: is the supported browsing surface of the open package Obsidian, or plain files and generated pages?

**Status:** open
**Source:** the Human, 2026-10-31 (consolidation session, [AHA-0017](AHA-0017)) - raised as an open question

## Idea

The knowledge is markdown in folders; the question is what the open package treats as its supported browsing surface:

- **Obsidian** - graph view, live linking, local browsing; obliges format constraints (link conventions, folder shape) and a stance on plugins and publishing.
- **The classic way** - plain files, generated projections, any editor; obliges nothing but forgoes the graph.

The choice is a decision to make on a panel ([idea-decision-panels](idea-decision-panels)) when it blocks; until then both remain possible because the format stays plain markdown.
