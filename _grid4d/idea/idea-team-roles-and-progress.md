# Team roles and progress

> Team work in a story needs described roles and visible progress: what each role is for and owes the story, and where the team stands.

**Status:** open
**Source:** the Human, 2026-10-31 (consolidation session, [AHA-0017](AHA-0017))

## Idea

- **Described roles.** The roles of a story team (author, reviewer, witness and peers) each get a short description: what the role is for, what it owes the story, what it may decide. Today the roles live in the method's practice and its story-team log; the description should be minimal and travel with the collaboration capability when it graduates.
- **Visible progress.** The team's progress within a story - which seats did what, which requirements hold, what waits on whom - is one of the decision panels ([idea-decision-panels](idea-decision-panels)), not a session-private view.

**Where it stands.** The machinery is implemented in the method: its collaboration subepic runs story teams with a participant registry (author, reviewer seats), addressed rows with deadlines, review verdicts and session liveness. This idea carries the roles described as minimal, portable text and the progress view as a panel - what graduates keeps both.
