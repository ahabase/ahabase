# AHA-0035: Standard workspace infrastructure and workspace-root worktrees

> Standard workspace infrastructure and workspace-root worktrees

**Type:** default
**Epic:** [AHABASE](AHABASE)
**Status:** in_progress
**Blueprint:** [implementation-story-blueprint](implementation-story-blueprint)
**Follows:** AHA-0034

## Goal

The consumer playground (AHA-0031) revealed an invention: the standalone engine mints story worktrees inside the Epic repository (`story.py` computes `epic.root / "_worktrees"` and injects a `_worktrees/` line into the consumer's `.gitignore` to keep it out of git status), while the proven evolucean topology keeps every special folder at the workspace root, outside all repositories. The Human ruling of 2026-11-22 sets the standard: the playground workspace carries the full folder set - `_config`, `_data`, `_generated`, `_inbox`, `_temp`, `_wikis`, `_worktrees` - at its root, worktrees included. This story unifies the standalone engine on that proven topology: worktrees mint under `<workspace>/_worktrees/<epic>/<STORY>`, the gitignore injection retires, and the workspace bootstrap creates and documents the seven folders. The engine already resolves its data dir as `<workspace>/_data/<epic>` (`get_data_dir` in config.py, the ported layout), so the story generalizes an existing seam rather than inventing one. The declared next story (Human, 2026-11-22) migrates wiki page generation incl. GitHub wiki publishing from the evolucean CLI (priority row 7) - the `_wikis/` folder is the home this story prepares, the capability itself is out of scope.

Serves [AHABASE-Priorities](AHABASE-Priorities) rows 3 (grid4d infrastructure) and 8 (story capability, the worktree leg); the follow-up story serves row 7.

## Requirements

### R1: workspace-root worktrees

`story.py` mints every story worktree under `<workspace>/_worktrees/<epic-name>/<STORY>` - the proven evolucean placement. Nothing worktree-shaped lands inside an Epic repository anymore: `_ignore_worktrees()` (the gitignore injection) is retired from the mint path, and the already-injected artifacts of the old layout are cleaned where they exist (the playground's scratch repo carries an injected `.gitignore` line and an empty `_worktrees/` directory - measured at mint: no worktree contents to relocate, the completed SCRATCH-0009/0010 worktrees are already gone).

**Acceptance Criterion:** a story minted in the playground creates its worktree at `<workspace>/_worktrees/<epic>/<STORY>`; the Epic repository contains no `_worktrees` and no injected ignore entry; both states measured (mint output path + repository tree listing), both polarities rehearsed in the suite.

### R2: the seven standard folders at the workspace root

The engine creates and recognizes the seven standard folders - `_config`, `_data`, `_generated`, `_inbox`, `_temp`, `_wikis`, `_worktrees` - at the workspace root, each with its consumer purpose (configuration, engine state, generated projections, external inputs, scratch space, wiki copies, story worktrees; `_data` already proven by `get_data_dir` in config.py). The workspace bootstrap path (the playground's `bootstrap.sh` and the engine command it drives) creates the set in a fresh workspace; folders a consumer deletes reappear on the next bootstrap run, and no Epic repository ever receives any of the seven.

**Acceptance Criterion:** after bootstrap in a fresh workspace, all seven folders exist at the workspace root and the Epic repositories stay clean of them; the suite measures the set as a census (all seven named, none inside a repo).

### R3: playground material rebase

The playground carries the new layout end to end: `bootstrap.sh` creates the seven folders, the CLAUDE.md workspace template documents them and the workspace-level worktree flow, `scenarios.md` walks the worktree path at its new location, and the pinned test paths (`r2-seam.sh` expecting `repos/messy/_worktrees/...`, `r4-arms.sh` editing `_worktrees/$NEXT`) move to `<workspace>/_worktrees/<epic>/<STORY>`. The messy-repo scenario keeps its spirit: registration stays the consumer's own act.

**Acceptance Criterion:** no playground document or test references an in-repo `_worktrees` path (grep over the playground tree is empty); the playground tests run green against the new layout in a fresh bootstrapped workspace.

### R4: suite and cold CI before public push

The story suite measures R1-R3 with rehearsed rows (every leg standalone, both polarities - the AHA-0034 lesson), and the artifact's own full test suite runs cold on the built export (fresh venv, wheel install, full pytest) before any public push - the standing public-push gate.

**Acceptance Criterion:** story suite green; cold CI green with the passed count recorded in this story; the public repository receives the merge only after both.

### R_team: Improve team-work quality (standing requirement)

Reviewers take seats through `trust join` (the invitation carrying the exact command went out on mint day); the review runs from copies, verdicts carry the tip flag, quorum per the review gate.

**Acceptance Criterion:** the review record shows registered seats and verdicts at pinned tips.

### R_docs: End-to-end documentation (standing requirement)

The consumer-facing docs state the workspace layout and the worktree flow at their new location; the story ledger documents the ruling and its why.

**Acceptance Criterion:** playground CLAUDE.md template and scenarios name the seven folders and the workspace worktree path.

### R_issues: Log all issues and ideas (standing requirement)

Newly named recurring problems surface as issue documents; the counters question (the standalone engine mints story IDs by scan, evolucean claims them on a counters branch - robust under concurrent mints) is recorded here as a follow-up candidate unless the Human adds it to scope.

**Acceptance Criterion:** the story completion leaves no unnamed recurring problem encountered during it.

## Acceptance Criteria

The aggregate closure: a fresh consumer workspace bootstrapped from this package carries the seven standard folders at its root, mints story worktrees outside every repository, and no injected ignore entry or in-repo `_worktrees` survives anywhere in the engine's output - measured by the suite (R1-R3), guarded by cold CI before the public push (R4), reviewed from copies by registered seats (R_team), and documented for the consumer (R_docs).
