## Goal

The Epic's direction accumulated faster than its documents. Three sets of the Human's notes (2026-10-31) carry a component inventory, a value-and-capability vision, and open process questions - none of it captured in `idea/`. The foundation roadmap that steers the Epic still describes the state after its second story; sixteen stories are complete. The architecture idea that started the package has been consumed by decisions its text does not know. And the Epic ontology holds every concept at the Epic level, so the first concrete capability (the safe) already sits one level above where the structure that proved itself in the method (subepics for a narrow focus) says a capability belongs.

This story consolidates without touching code or moving ontology files: every shaped idea is captured in `idea/` under the new `idea-` prefix; the ontology's shape is decided and declared - architecture concepts stay in the Epic ontology, a concrete capability's concepts belong in its subepic's ontology, with the safe as the first subepic of a follow-up story; the roadmap is reconciled into this repository's own extract; and new issue and idea files gain the `issue-`/`idea-` prefixes.

### R2: The ontology shape is decided and declared

Two rulings of the Human, 2026-10-31, recorded by meaning:

- **Shape** - the Epic ontology carries the architecture of ahabase only (the structural concepts: Epic, Trust Zone, Boundary, Door, Plugin, Registry, Portability, Lineage, Consumer, and peers). A concrete capability's concepts live in that capability's subepic ontology; the safe is the first of them. This dissolves the unpleasant exception that the Epic's first part, the safe, grew at the Epic level.
- **Admission** - a concept enters an ontology only when it is robust, minimal, and needed by already-agreed implementation. The ontology shape itself is reconsidered again when the format and workflow graduate.

The moves themselves are a follow-up story (create the subepics, move the capability blueprints, register the subepics, teach the verify gates where each index lives); wiki links are name-only, so the moves are cheap for links and expensive only for the registry and the gates.

**Acceptance Criterion:** AHABASE.md Rules gain the two rules above; `implementation-ahabase-architecture-blueprint` records the target structure (which concepts stay Epic-level, which capability concepts move, the safe subepic first) and names the follow-up story as its executor; the git diff of this story touches no file under `_grid4d/ontology/` and creates no `subepic/` directory.
