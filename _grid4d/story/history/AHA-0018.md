### R4: The trust-zone blueprint is amended to six zones

[ontology-trust-zone-blueprint](ontology-trust-zone-blueprint) is rewritten per the Human's rulings of 2026-10-31:

- **Six zones by receiver distance, machine receivers included:** 0 Public, 1 Users, 2 Shared, 3 Core, 4 Personal, 5 Vault. The distance chain: the person alone (5) - the person and their own instances (4) - plus a circle named by the owner's judgment (3) - plus a group defined by a standing rule (2) - plus anyone holding the package through its channels (1) - plus anyone (0).
- **Guards per rung:** zones 0-4 are repository grants at the epic level, owner-relative; zone 1 is a channel grant governed by the package; zone 5 is a representation guarantee - values in the store outside git, references in documents, reveal by a person only, the agent guard and output watch keeping values out of context.
- **Shared** is an audience defined by a standing rule - earned community standing, team or org-unit membership, partnership; **Core** is an audience defined by the owner's judgment - named persons or units, case by case. A company-wide internal epic is Shared (employment is the widest rule inside the company's bubble).
- **Personal** is the owning scope only - a person's machine-only epics, an organization's epics restricted below its baseline rule; **Vault** holds values, never documents - a document containing a secret does not exist, it holds a reference. Documents occupy zones 0-4.
- **Zones are owner-relative:** an individual climbs a trust ladder; a company runs an isolated installation (its enterprise perimeter) and shares by its organizational structure, not into the public community. Bridges outward are decisions, delegable per the owner's policy.
- **Document zone is a travel classification:** how far content may go when extracted or published; an epic's effective zone is the innermost of its documents; a document may sit only in an epic shared no farther than its zone allows. Crossings are whole-epic decisions or extracts, never per-file grants.
- **TLP 2.0 mapping is many-to-one:** CLEAR (0), GREEN (1, 2), AMBER+STRICT (3), RED (4, 5) - a zone implies a TLP label, not a one-to-one pair.
- **Constraints kept:** outward is a decision; inward is earned, through channels before repositories; Never back; external parties are zoned too; Vault reaches no external processor.
- The Example section gains practical examples per zone, generic ones only - this blueprint is Public-perimeter content, so no real organization, employer, or account name appears in it.

**Acceptance Criterion:** the blueprint carries the six zones with the receiver chain, the per-rung guards, the Shared/Core and Personal/Vault distinctions, the owner-relative reading, the many-to-one TLP mapping, and a practical Example per zone; no real organization, employer, or account name appears.
