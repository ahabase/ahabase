# AHA-0004: ahabase ontology: the open foundation - concepts of the package, the safe, trust zones and boundaries; the core extends it

> ahabase is the base, evolucean a method that extends it: the big picture of the Epic's concepts, the split of competence, the plan of the move and of the turned inheritance, and the concepts the next steps build on.

**Type:** default
**Epic:** [AHABASE](AHABASE)
**Status:** completed
**Blueprint:** [implementation-story-blueprint](implementation-story-blueprint)

## Goal

The Epic had no ontology: its blueprints had nothing to implement, and the concepts the open zone needs lived in the closed method or as a short extract in the identity. Shaped with the Human on 2026-10-25: the premise that the value of knowledge grows while the cost of work, software included, falls; ahabase as the tool to build a knowledge base where knowledge is shared under control or protected; architecturally ahabase is the core - the base - and evolucean a method that inherits from it. This story writes the skeleton of the whole Epic, the split of competence with its criterion, the plan of moving concepts into the open zone and of turning the inheritance, and defines the concepts of the next steps (trust and boundaries, the platform). The title keeps the word core as the story was named before the ruling.

## Requirements

### R1: Big picture

The ontology index names every layer of the Epic - primitives, knowledge structure (the grid4d format), trust and boundaries, platform, the basis of collaboration - and marks which layers are defined now and which when they are built.

**Acceptance Criterion:** `_grid4d/ontology/00-ahabase-ontology-index.md` exists with Purpose, Taxonomy (all layers with state), Vocabulary, Relations, Constraints and passes verification.

### R2: Competence of base and method

The split is recorded with its reasons: the boundary criterion (structure that software can verify belongs to the base, how to work well to a method), never back (nothing returns from the open zone), collaboration in three layers (open basis, paid organizational layer, a method's tuned playbooks), and the direction (a method extends the base). Naming follows it: ahabase is the base, evolucean a method; the current documents no longer call evolucean the core.

**Acceptance Criterion:** The rules are in the index Constraints and the identity; no current document of the Epic calls evolucean the core (released stories excluded).

### R3: The move and the turned inheritance

The architecture blueprint carries the argument (the premise, commoditizing the complement, proven open-base shapes and the failure mode to avoid), the way the closed part adapts (strangler fig, branch by abstraction, parallel change) and the steps and gate of turning the inheritance; the identity records the current `Inherits` line as a known exception until then.

**Acceptance Criterion:** The blueprint has a section Base and method with the steps and the gate; the identity names the exception.

### R4: Concepts of the next steps

Layer 2 (Trust Zone, Boundary, Safe Value, Reference, Store, Manifest, Consumer, Shadow Run, Rotation, Agent Guard, Output Watch, Scan Finding) and layer 3 (Door, Registry, Plugin, Health Check) are defined, one blueprint each; the implementation blueprints name the concepts they implement; the identity's extract of zones is replaced by the ontology as the source of truth.

**Acceptance Criterion:** Sixteen concept blueprints exist and pass verification; each is in the index Vocabulary; both implementation blueprints link concepts in Implements; the identity has no Concepts in brief section.

### R5: Links point inward

**Acceptance Criterion:** The link check of the Epic finds no link that leaves the repository and no closed-zone document name, except the listed generated lines tied to their issues.

### R_team: Team-work quality

**Acceptance Criterion:** One review round on both instances with a 45-minute deadline, content frozen during the round (the lesson of the previous story); every verdict recorded with its commit; friction logged.

### R_docs: Documentation

**Acceptance Criterion:** Index, concepts, blueprints and identity consistent with each other.

### R_issues: Issues

**Acceptance Criterion:** Every problem met is fixed or logged.
