## Notes

Created: 2026-10-30

Direction of the Human, 2026-10-30, recorded by meaning:

1. **Safe for all sensitive information.** The condition of Ahabase's success is the absolute safety of every user's sensitive information. LLMs have huge capabilities, so before they are let loose, sensitive information must stay safe, and the first step of Ahabase is the safe module for handling sensitive data. Today the safe covers secrets only ([implementation-ahabase-safe-blueprint](implementation-ahabase-safe-blueprint): "one interface for secrets"; the threat model is on credentials) and the Rule "Safe first" names credentials. Still missing: personal data, private notes, organization-confidential data, and the content of closed plug-and-play Epics. Proposed mechanisms:
   - a sensitivity label set by the owner that travels with the content;
   - an egress check on every channel, above all the LLM prompt: the zone must allow the processor, else the content is masked or refused;
   - references instead of values, as for secrets;
   - consent enforced by software, not by a seat's judgement.

   **Scope ruling of 2026-10-30, later the same day: for now the safe's scope stays on credentials including tokens**, which is critical and already implemented; the widening above is the direction and a later step, not the scope of the first release. The safe was exercised the same day in an isolated run (92 of 92 tests; an interactive walk-through with a made-up token: the value never reached the output, the manifest or any file; an unbound program got nothing; a bound consumer received only the named variable; a changed consumer file lost its binding; reveal, bind and delete refused without an owner at a terminal).

   Live case of the same day: a seat refused to send private Apple Notes to the LLM step without the Human's consent in its own session. That was a judgement; no mechanism enforced it.

2. **Open tools, closed knowledge.** Software is open, for trust and for fast development. The greatest value is one's own knowledge, so plug-and-play Epics stay closed, and each owner decides whether to share at the level of a community, an organization or an individual. The protection mechanism itself (trust zones, the sharing gate) belongs to the open core (Kerckhoffs's principle); only the knowledge and the keys are closed. LLM providers count as zoned external parties. AHABASE.md Vision already holds "the mechanism is open and what is protected is knowledge" (2026-10-25); this sharpens it.

3. **Reciprocity: sharing multiplies.** Open software is a gift to everyone, but the greatest value must go to altruists. The ability to share and collaborate multiplies what Ahabase gives the one who shares, so altruists gain a competitive advantage over the selfish.
   - Mechanism: trust zones earned from a verifiable record. What counts is the value delivered to others (Karma of usage by others), not the volume shared.
   - A non-sharer keeps the full tool, without the multiplier.
   - Guard against gaming (sybil identities, rings, spam) with vouching and probation; the rules belong to the communities.
   - Prior art: copyleft; Axelrod's tit-for-tat and BitTorrent; Ostrom's governance of the commons; Benkler's commons-based peer production.

   AHABASE.md Vision already holds "altruism that brings a differential advantage" (2026-10-25); this adds the multiplier and the measure.

4. **OPEN QUESTION, evolucean open or closed.** The Human said on 2026-10-30 "open Epics ahabase and evolucean". AHABASE.md Overview (2026-10-25) says evolucean is a method, "a closed, optional plugin" on the open base. The options put to the Human:
   - (A) open evolucean whole, which requires cleaning its knowledge of instance and company context;
   - (B) keep the 2026-10-25 split: the open base, with evolucean as a closed method;
   - (C) something between, for example an open CLI with closed method tuning (playbooks, patterns).

   **Decided 2026-10-30: option C.** The Human chose it after the measured situation: the evolucean repository is private, about 200 of its files name a closed Epic, and the same holds in its git history. Software becomes public by graduating into Ahabase. The Human refined the same day: evolucean is open only to the community of its developers, shared in their trust zone and never public, while ahabase is fully public. Landed in AHABASE.md Overview (Incubator and proven selection).

5. **Incubator and proven selection.** Evolucean is the prototype of new capabilities, and Ahabase is the selection of proven ones. Assessed as a sound pattern (Fedora and RHEL, Chrome Canary and Stable, the Apache Incubator), on one condition: one-way graduation without copies, where evolucean deletes its copy and consumes Ahabase. This is already the Rule "One place per capability". Proposed graduation criteria, checked by software: an ontology and a blueprint; tests with red controls; proven use (Karma, sensors); no instance or company detail (containment); named seams; and the Human's confirmation. The alternatives weighed were one codebase with an experimental flag, and a rewrite of Ahabase from scratch. A candidate for the first decision record of the Epic (with the decision method that evolucean is developing).

6. **Open-source business models (research input for where the open/closed boundary lies).** Two axes:
   - (i) where the boundary lies: in the architecture (open core with closed extensions; or a closed core with an open plugin ecosystem, which is a proprietary platform and not open source, Obsidian for example), in a service (hosting, sync), in support and certification, in the licence (dual licensing; source-available licences such as BSL, SSPL and FSL, where hard licence changes tended to provoke community forks such as OpenTofu and Valkey), or in funding (foundations, sponsors, grants);
   - (ii) who pays and for what.

   For Ahabase the existing traces point to a hybrid: an open core with organization-specific extension points (the `organization` signer adapter), plus a boundary drawn by trust zones rather than by licence. The latest licence changes should be checked against current sources before any decision.
